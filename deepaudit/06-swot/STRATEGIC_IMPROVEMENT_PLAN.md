# Strategic Improvement Plan

## 0–72 hours

Contain DATA-001, freeze release, preserve evidence, notify accountable owners, assess credentials/sessions and distribution.

## 1–2 weeks

Restore frontend provenance and test execution; correct PM2/cluster/readiness/migration invariants; disable or unify privileged alternate routes; make reset tokens at-most-once; isolate export network access.

## 2–6 weeks

Repair cover persistence, template policy, webhook replay, dependency advisories and gateway transport. Establish disposable MariaDB/Firebase emulator CI and a generated route registry.

## 6–12 weeks

Deliver missing templates with a canonical renderer/catalog, complete parity automation, correct mobile/accessibility behavior, consolidate schema/repository authority, and remove proven stale code/assets.

Success is measured by release gates, not elapsed time.
